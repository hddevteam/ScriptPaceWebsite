Video setup & product support

From camera placement to your next clean take.

Find quick-start steps for filming with external cameras or screen recorders, Remote pairing, purchase restoration, and common troubleshooting answers.

Quick start for video shoots

  1. Open ScriptPace on Mac, load a supported Markdown or plain-text script, and drag the floating panel right below your camera lens.
  2. Use system speech to hear the phrasing first, or rehearse on iPhone.
  3. Open Remote on iPhone and scan the QR code on Mac so paragraph control stays off-camera.
  4. Start Voice Follow alongside your camera, Continuity Camera, OBS, or screen recorder.

Mac prompting & built-in recording

Mac includes floating near-lens prompting, system speech, Voice Follow, and side-by-side read-only transcript comparison. Built-in recording adds savable capture presets to jump into recording mode anytime, automatic script-based archiving, and export presets that composite directly or split into multi-layer assets (arriving with Ultra on the App Store).

iPhone Remote

Once paired, Remote mirrors the Mac script and active paragraph. Even when presenting full-screen slides or looking straight into a camera lens, you can adjust paragraphs silently without touching the mouse.

Apple Watch

Apple Watch is a free Remote companion with no separate purchase. Turn the Digital Crown or tap previous and next paragraph controls while standing or demonstrating hands-on.

Purchases and restore

Monthly Pro and Annual Pro are auto-renewable subscriptions and unlock ScriptPace prompting, Voice Follow, voice practice, and Remote only—they do not include built-in screen recording. Annual Ultra and Ultra Lifetime are coming soon to the App Store; final entitlements, prices, and trial eligibility will appear on the purchase page. Sign in with the same Apple Account and choose Restore Purchases on additional devices. Apple allows one introductory offer per subscription group.

Permissions & system speech

Voice Follow relies on Apple speech capabilities and installed language resources. Allow microphone and speech recognition permissions on first launch and verify your OS meets the App Store requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Common answers before and during a shoot.

When will built-in Mac screen recording be available in ScriptPace?

Coming soon. ScriptPace includes the floating Mac teleprompter, Voice Follow, voice practice, transcript comparison, and iPhone / Apple Watch Remote. Built-in Mac screen, camera, and audio recording—along with automatic script-based archiving, production video import, and the Export Editor—is releasing soon on the App Store.

How do I film videos with ScriptPace using a camera, OBS, or another screen recorder?

Open your script on Mac, place the floating ScriptPace window right below your webcam or camera lens, turn on Voice Follow, and start your camera, Continuity Camera, OBS, or QuickTime recording as usual. Keep iPhone Remote or Apple Watch nearby to step between paragraphs silently off-camera.

Why does iPhone Remote not show as connected?

Confirm ScriptPace is open on both Mac and iPhone, scan the QR code currently shown on Mac again, allow local-network discovery permissions, and keep both devices on a compatible network. If the connection drops, return to Remote and scan again.

What should I do if a paragraph shifts out of place when I improvise during a shoot?

There is no need to reach for your mouse on camera. Use previous or next paragraph on iPhone Remote or turn the Apple Watch Digital Crown off-camera to align the active paragraph and keep speaking.

Why is Pro not visible on another device after purchase?

Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, then choose Restore Purchases on the other device. Universal Purchase shares the Apple entitlement without creating a separate server account.

Does ScriptPace save or upload my microphone audio or recorded videos?

Voice Follow and voice practice never save raw microphone audio. The upcoming built-in recorder keeps captured takes and imported production videos locally on your Mac—removing an imported clip never deletes the external file—and never uploads media to ScriptPace servers.
