Video setup & product support

From camera placement to your next clean take.

Find quick-start steps for filming with external cameras or screen recorders, Remote pairing, free trials, and purchase restoration.

Quick start for video shoots

  1. Open ScriptPace on Mac, load a supported Markdown or plain-text script, and drag the floating panel right below your camera lens.
  2. Use system speech to hear the phrasing first, or rehearse offline on iPhone.
  3. Open Remote on iPhone and scan the QR code on Mac for silent off-camera control—solo or with a teammate.
  4. Start Voice Follow alongside your camera, Continuity Camera, OBS, or screen recorder.

Mac prompting, layered capture & local MCP

Mac includes floating near-lens prompting, system speech, Voice Follow, and side-by-side read-only transcript comparison. Planned for the upcoming Ultra release on the App Store, built-in window and region presets let you record directly on your daily work Mac—exporting either a finished movie or separated screen, alpha camera, audio, synchronized .srt, and word-timing JSON, plus local stdio MCP (--mcp) so Codex, GitHub Copilot, or Claude can assist with rough-cut markers and preset exports under explicit local folder permission.

iPhone Remote

Once paired, Remote mirrors the Mac script and active paragraph. When presenting full-screen slides or looking straight into a camera lens, tap previous or next paragraph silently from off camera.

Apple Watch

Apple Watch is included as a free Remote companion. Turn the Digital Crown or tap previous and next paragraph controls while standing or demonstrating hands-on.

Free trials, purchases, and restore

Monthly Pro and Annual Pro are auto-renewable subscriptions with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers (unlocking ScriptPace prompting, Voice Follow, voice practice, and Remote; built-in screen recording is excluded). Annual Ultra (offering a 1-month free trial) and Ultra Lifetime are coming soon to the App Store; final entitlements, prices, and trial eligibility appear on the purchase page. Sign in with the same Apple Account and choose Restore Purchases on additional devices. Apple allows one introductory offer per subscription group.

Permissions & system speech

Voice Follow relies on Apple speech capabilities and installed language resources. Allow microphone and speech recognition permissions on first launch and verify your OS meets the App Store requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Common answers before and during a shoot.

When will built-in Mac screen recording be available in ScriptPace?

Coming soon. ScriptPace includes the floating Mac teleprompter, Voice Follow, voice practice, transcript comparison, and iPhone / Apple Watch Remote (with a 7-day free trial on Pro). Built-in Mac screen, camera, and audio recording—along with layered media export, synchronized SRT and word-timing JSON, local MCP integration for Codex, GitHub Copilot, and Claude, and script-folder archiving—is releasing soon with Ultra (including a 1-month free trial on Annual Ultra).

How do I film videos with ScriptPace using a camera, OBS, or another screen recorder?

Open your script on Mac, place the floating ScriptPace window right below your webcam or camera lens, turn on Voice Follow, and start your camera, Continuity Camera, OBS, or QuickTime recording as usual. Keep iPhone Remote or Apple Watch nearby to step between paragraphs silently off camera.

Why does iPhone Remote not show as connected?

Confirm ScriptPace is open on both Mac and iPhone, scan the QR code currently shown on Mac again, allow local-network discovery permissions, and keep both devices on a compatible network. If the connection drops, return to Remote and scan again.

How does ScriptPace handle live improvisation, retakes, or paragraph jumps during a shoot?

Voice Follow tracks your spoken script content automatically: it pauses on the current line while you elaborate around your draft, catches up when you return or jump paragraphs, and rewinds on your rerecord command. For manual off-camera control, tap previous or next paragraph on iPhone Remote or turn the Apple Watch Digital Crown.

Why is Pro not visible on another device after purchase?

Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, then choose Restore Purchases on the other device. Universal Purchase shares the Apple entitlement without creating a separate server account.

Does ScriptPace save or upload my microphone audio or recorded videos?

Voice Follow and voice practice never save raw microphone audio. The upcoming built-in recorder keeps captured takes and imported production videos locally on your Mac—removing an imported clip never deletes the external file—and never uploads media to ScriptPace servers.
