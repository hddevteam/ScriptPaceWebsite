Core highlight · Voice Follow teleprompter

You prepared what you want to say. The teleprompter should not become another thing to manage.

Fixed-speed scrollers move too fast, so you chase the words—or too slowly, so you wait. A thoughtful pause, an unscripted example, or a quick retake pulls your eyes off the lens while you hunt for your line.

ScriptPace makes the path calmer: place the floating window right beneath your Mac camera so your voice moves the script forward, pauses naturally when you pause, steps back when a take needs another try, and pairs with read-only transcript comparison afterwards.

Setup 01 · Talking-head & camera shoots

Keep eye contact near the lens with any camera or OBS.

Place the floating Mac teleprompter just beneath your webcam, Continuity Camera, or mirrorless lens. Voice Follow advances as you speak and waits when you pause.

  • Natural eye line without chasing a fixed-speed scroll
  • Adjust paragraphs silently from iPhone Remote or Apple Watch—no reaching for the mouse mid-take
Setup 02 · Online courses & tutorials

Rehearse long lessons, then review the spoken transcript.

Listen to system speech first to catch awkward phrasing, rehearse offline on iPhone, then record on Mac and compare your read-only spoken transcript against the script.

  • Connected prep loop: hear it, practice on iPhone, deliver on Mac
  • Side-by-side read-only transcript comparison without automatic script rewriting
Setup 03 · Software walkthroughs Built-in capture coming soon

Present your screen while takes archive automatically by script.

Capture a full display, window, or selected portion, start prompting automatically when the countdown hits zero, and automatically file every take under its script folder.

  • Separate system audio, microphone, and camera capture with instant multi-take filing
  • Picture-in-picture overlays, synchronized SRT subtitle export, and finished video import
