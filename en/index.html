Core highlight · Voice Follow teleprompter

You prepared what you want to say. The teleprompter should not become another thing to manage.

Fixed-speed scrollers move too fast, so you chase the words—or too slowly, so you wait. A thoughtful pause, an unscripted example, or a quick retake pulls your eyes off the lens while you hunt for your line.

ScriptPace makes the path calmer: place the floating window right beneath your Mac camera so your voice moves the script forward, pauses naturally when you pause, steps back when a take needs another try, and pairs with read-only transcript comparison afterwards.

Setup 01 · Talking-head & camera shoots

Near-lens Voice Follow with any camera or OBS.

Place the floating Mac teleprompter right beneath your webcam, Continuity Camera, or mirrorless lens. Voice Follow moves at your real speaking pace, waits naturally when you pause to think, and picks right back up after an unscripted aside.

  • Real-time Voice Follow near the lens—no chasing or waiting on a fixed-speed scroll
  • Adjust paragraphs silently from iPhone Remote or Apple Watch—no reaching for the mouse mid-take
Setup 02 · Online courses & tutorials

Rehearse long lessons, then review the spoken transcript.

Listen to system speech first to catch awkward phrasing, rehearse offline on iPhone, then record on Mac and compare your read-only spoken transcript against the script.

  • Connected prep loop: hear it, practice on iPhone, deliver on Mac
  • Side-by-side read-only transcript comparison without automatic script rewriting
Setup 03 · Software walkthroughs

Plan capture presets for repetitive framing and spec checks.

Screen demos often mean prepping a script, setting capture bounds, and verifying export specs every time. The upcoming ScriptPace recorder is planned to pair Voice Follow with reusable capture and export presets.

  • Full-screen, window, region, and picture-in-picture camera overlay templates with saved recording presets
  • Standardized export presets to composite directly or split into layered media with synchronized SRT subtitles

Coming soon · Built-in Mac recording, presets & archive

Planned: natural prompting, saved recording presets, and spec-ready exports.

The upcoming Mac workflow is designed to bring Voice Follow prompting, reusable recording presets, script-based archiving, and flexible export presets together locally.

Capture templates & saved presets

Save your recording preset and enter capture mode anytime.

Choose full-display, single-window, custom-region, audio-only, or camera-only templates—and save your capture bounds, audio sources, and camera overlay layout as presets for one-click recall. When the countdown hits zero, capture and natural prompting start together, with automatic pause trimming and instant multi-take filing.

Script archive & Export Presets

Export presets that composite directly or split into multi-layer assets.

Every script-linked take files automatically into a matching script folder—alongside imported finished production cuts (marked Imported). Built-in templates (Web & Devices, Small File, High Quality ProRes, Subject Cutout) and custom export presets lock in your delivery specs: composite a finished movie directly, or split into layered media assets (screen, camera overlay, audio, and synchronized .srt subtitles) for any workflow.

01 · Recording templates & presets

Frame once, jump into recording mode anytime.

Save capture mode, display/window/region selection, system audio, 48 kHz lossless microphone, and picture-in-picture layout as reusable presets instead of reconfiguring before every shoot.

02 · Script archive & video import

Keep raw takes and final cuts in one script folder.

Recordings automatically organize by active script, and you can import external production videos right alongside raw takes with favorites and notes—removing an imported clip never deletes the external file.

03 · Export presets: composite or layered

Meet delivery specs for quick publishing or NLE edits.

Apply built-in templates or saved export presets to standardize codecs, resolution, and frame rate—compositing a finished clip directly or exporting multi-layer media assets and synchronized subtitle files.

Note: Built-in Mac screen, camera, and audio recording and the Export workspace arrive with the upcoming Ultra release on the App Store.
