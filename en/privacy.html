Privacy policy

Local-first and transparent.

ScriptPace is a local-first video teleprompter and creative tool. Your scripts, practice sessions, and local media stay on your own devices.

Effective date: September 6, 2026

1. What we process

ScriptPace lets you use teleprompting and Remote features across Mac, iPhone, and Apple Watch. Scripts, reading position, practice transcripts, and pairing state may move between your devices when you actively use those features. Unless the product explicitly says otherwise, we do not upload this content to a ScriptPace-operated server.

Voice Follow and voice practice use speech capabilities supplied by Apple. Processing can depend on your device, operating system, language resources, and permissions; the exact behavior is provided by the system available on your device.

2. Local storage & privacy commitments

  • ScriptPace does not require an application account.
  • ScriptPace has no advertising tracker, self-hosted analytics, or self-hosted telemetry service.
  • Voice Follow and voice practice do not store raw microphone audio or upload raw microphone audio to a ScriptPace server; in future releases where you explicitly initiate screen, camera, or audio recording—or import external production videos into the Export list—captured source takes, imported file references, and exported files remain stored locally on your Mac (removing an imported item never deletes the external file) and are never uploaded to a ScriptPace server.
  • ScriptPace does not use your scripts for automatic rewriting, advertising profiles, or training a self-hosted model.

3. Apple services and purchases

App Store, StoreKit, Apple Account, and system speech services are provided by Apple and governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policy. ScriptPace reads the current purchase entitlement available to the device so your purchased entitlement can be restored across supported Apple platforms; ScriptPace never receives your Apple Account password.

4. Retention, deletion, and device control

The storage location for scripts, practice records, transcripts, and local media depends on the current app version and device system. Use the deletion, cleanup, or uninstall paths provided by the app to manage local data. If you need to understand how information in a support request is handled, contact us through the support email.

5. Children and sensitive content

ScriptPace is not a medical, legal, or safety decision tool. Do not enter another person's sensitive information, confidential material, or protected content without the necessary authorization. You are responsible for having the rights needed to use your scripts and speech content.

6. Changes

If our data practices, third-party services, or product boundary changes, we will update this policy and the App Store Connect privacy answers before the change is released. Material changes may be called out in an appropriate product page or release note.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, deletion requests, or data inquiries, contact: support email.
